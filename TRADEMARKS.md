- **Forks and modified versions.** You may fork the Apache-licensed code, but you may **not** use the Marks as the name of your fork or in a way that suggests your fork is the official project or is endorsed by TekDatum. ## Package naming

The official packages are `simlar` (public PyPI) and `simlar-engine` (private index). Please do not publish packages under these names or confusingly similar names. Name plugins and extensions so they're clearly third-party (e.g., `your-name-for-simlar` rather than `simlar-official`).

## General guidelines

- Use the Marks only to refer to the genuine project or your truthful relationship to it.
- Don't use them in a misleading, disparaging, or unlawful way.
